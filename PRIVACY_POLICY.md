# Privacy Policy for TodoDroid

**Last updated: June 2026**

## Data Collection
TodoDroid stores all your data **locally on your device only**. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

## What We Store
- Task titles and descriptions
- Note content and formatting
- App preferences (dark mode, sort settings)

## Data Storage
All data is stored in a local JSON file on your device. You can backup this file to your Google Drive or any cloud service manually through the Settings menu.

## Permissions
- **Microphone**: Used only for speech-to-text feature. No audio is recorded or stored.
- **Camera**: Used only to set photo backgrounds for notes. No photos are uploaded anywhere.
- **Storage**: Used to save backups and export notes.

## Third-Party Services
TodoDroid does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
